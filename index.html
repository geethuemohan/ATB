Who We Are

A Specialist Advisory Practice for India–UAE Market Entry & Cross-Border Growth

ATB Corporate is a specialist advisory firm focused on market entry and cross-border business expansion between India, the UAE and international markets. We work with entrepreneurs, family businesses, SMEs and international companies looking to structure, establish and grow operations across both regions.

Our team brings together expertise in UAE and India regulatory frameworks, tax structuring, transaction advisory and market entry execution — giving clients a single, commercially aware advisory partner for complex cross-border mandates.

India–UAE Market Entry Advisory

Cross-Border Structuring

ADGM, DIFC, GIFT City, free zone and mainland structures designed for your commercial objectives, not just compliance.

Ongoing Advisory & Execution

Tax, accounting, compliance and strategic support as your cross-border operations grow.
